Common questions

Is Selah free?

Yes. Selah is free to download and use on iPhone and Android.

My highlights or notes are missing

Make sure you are signed in with the same Google or Apple account you used before. Guest data lives only on the device it was created on and does not sync between phones.

Explanations or Ask are not loading

These features need an internet connection. Check your connection, then close and reopen the app. If it keeps failing, email us with the verse or question you were trying.

How do I delete my account?

See Delete your Selah account for the in-app steps and the web request option.
